## 🚀 Our Mission (我们的使命)

At VisiGap, we are building the next generation of search engines. Our mission is to empower users to discover hidden connections and unlock profound insights from the vast expanse of digital information. We believe that by bridging the "gaps" in visual and textual data, we can create a more intuitive, intelligent, and insightful way to explore the world's knowledge.

(在 VisiGap，我们致力于构建下一代搜索引擎。我们的使命是帮助用户从浩瀚的数字信息中发现隐藏的关联，并解锁深刻的洞见。我们相信，通过弥合视觉与文本数据之间的“缝隙”，我们能够创造一种更直观、更智能、更有洞察力的知识探索方式。)

## ✨ What We Do (我们的项目)

Our organization hosts a collection of projects that together form the **VisiGap Engine**. Key repositories include:

*   🧠 **[core-engine](link-to-repo)**: The heart of VisiGap. This repository contains the core indexing, querying, and ranking algorithms. (技术栈: Rust, Python)
*   🌐 **[crawler](link-to-repo)**: Our distributed, high-performance web crawler for gathering data from across the web. (技术栈: Go)
*   🖼️ **[frontend-ui](link-to-repo)**: The beautiful, responsive, and user-friendly interface for interacting with the VisiGap Engine. (技术栈: React, TypeScript)
*   🤖 **[ml-models](link-to-repo)**: Home to our machine learning models for image recognition, natural language processing, and pattern detection. (技术栈: Python, PyTorch)

## 🤝 How to Contribute (如何贡献)

VisiGap is an open-source project, and we welcome contributions from everyone! Whether you are a developer, a designer, a writer, or just an enthusiastic user, there are many ways to get involved.

1.  **Explore our repositories**: Check out the `issues` tab in our project repositories. Look for issues tagged with `good first issue` or `help wanted`.
2.  **Read our guidelines**: Before you start, please read our [**Contributing Guidelines**](./CONTRIBUTING.md) to understand our workflow and best practices.
3.  **Join the discussion**: Have an idea or a question? Join our [**Community Discord/Slack**](link-to-community) to chat with the team and other contributors.

We value every contribution and are excited to build this future with you!
